How is the 2027 rating calculated?

Handicap strength uses relative handicap seed percentile across the entered field. Tournament performance uses an adjusted share: (points earned + 2) / (points available + 4), giving each golfer a neutral four-point prior. The rating is a weighted average of those two percentages. Missing handicaps are unranked. This rating is advisory and is not an official Handicap Index.
